SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA - NOT REAL MEASUREMENTS.

Every numeric measurement was replaced by a random draw from the mean and standard deviation of its
season x water-regime (x date, x index) group, clipped to the observed range. Plot layout and treatment
codes are kept so that the code runs end to end. Relationships between variables (e.g. NDVI vs bands,
yield vs canopy) are NOT preserved, so results computed on these files are meaningless.
